Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Pull-Up Club ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Pull-Up Club is designed with privacy in mind. The app collects minimal information necessary for it to function:

  • Workout Data: The app stores your workout information locally on your device, including:
    • Workout type and configuration
    • Number of sets and repetitions
    • Workout start and end times
  • No Personal Information: The app does not currently collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location.
  • No Account Data: The app does not currently require user accounts or registration, so no account information is collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

All workout data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database. This data is used solely to:

  • Display your workout history
  • Track your progress over time
  • Provide functionality within the app

The app does not currently transmit, share, or sell any of your data to third parties. By default, all data remains on your device and is not sent to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: The app may download fonts from Google Fonts to display text. This is handled by the Google Fonts service and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used in this app.

4. Data Storage and Security

Currently, all workout data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database file. This data is:

  • Stored in your device's secure application documents directory
  • Not accessible by other apps on your device
  • Not backed up to cloud services unless you have enabled device backups
  • Deleted when you uninstall the app

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your workout data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual workouts within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which will remove all stored data

Since all data is currently stored locally on your device, we do not have access to your data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

6. Children's Privacy

Pull-Up Club is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address the issue.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is currently stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can:

  • View all stored data within the app
  • Delete any or all data at any time
  • Export your data (if such functionality is added in the future)

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Issue Tracker.
