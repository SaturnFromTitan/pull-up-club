## 1.0.0

Struggling to increase your pull-up count? This app gives you a simple and effective training plan designed to help you double your maximum pull-ups. It works best if you are currently in the 5-12 repetition range. In only eight weeks, you can increase your maximum reps by 50-100% and finally break through your plateau.

- A training plan that works
- Clear, focused, and distraction-free user interface
- Workout history to track your progress
- Offline-first design so the app works reliably without internet reception
- All your data stays on your local device - no tracking, no account registration
- Nothing to hide - source code is available in a public GitHub repo
- Supports a wide range of iOS devices with varying screen sizes
